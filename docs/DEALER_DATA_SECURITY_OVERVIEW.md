# Your Customers' Data Is Safe — and It Stays Yours

**A plain-language overview for dealership partners**

Your customers trust you with their information. This is how we make sure that trust is never broken — and how we keep your store protected at the same time. No jargon, just straight answers to the questions that matter.

---

## First, the one thing to know

**You own your customers' data. We don't.**

You are the owner of every lead, conversation, and customer record. We are your technology provider — we run the software for you, but we never own your data, never sell it, never use it to train AI, and never use it to compete with you or reach out to your customers. That isn't just a promise; it's written into our agreement and built into how the system works.

---

## "Could our customers' information be lost or stolen?"

We protect it the way a bank protects an account.

- **It's encrypted** everywhere — while it travels and while it sits in storage. If anyone intercepted it, they'd get scrambled, unreadable data.
- **Each store is walled off from every other store.** Your customers, leads, conversations, and recordings can only be seen by your team. Another dealership on the platform physically cannot reach your data — the system blocks it at the deepest level, not just on the screen.
- **We keep less over time.** Sensitive items like call recordings and transcripts are automatically erased as they age, so there's simply less information sitting around to ever be at risk.

## "Could *you* (the platform) take our data?"

No. Only a very small number of our senior administrators can ever cross between dealerships, and that ability is granted one person at a time and logged. Your own staff can't reach another store's data, and no ordinary account can either. And contractually, we're barred from selling your data, training AI on it, or using it to compete with you. If you ever leave us, you get a full copy within a week and we delete everything within 30 days.

## "How do we know a customer agreed to be contacted?"

Every single lead comes with a built-in receipt. The moment a customer submits, we record exactly what they agreed to, when they agreed, and from what device — tied to that customer's record. So if a customer ever claims they didn't opt in, you have proof. And if a customer texts **STOP**, they're immediately removed from *all* contact — calls, texts, and emails — automatically.

## "Can a customer delete their own information?"

Yes — by themselves, right from your website, in a couple of minutes. They tap a button, confirm with a text message code to prove it's really them, and they can either download everything you have on them or have it permanently erased. No phone calls, no waiting on your staff. It's the kind of control today's customers expect, and it's a feature you can point to with pride.

## "Does this protect *us* legally?"

Yes — that's deliberate. Your customer-facing pages make clear that a number is an **estimate** unless you choose to make a **firm offer**. And even a firm offer only holds if the car checks out in person — if the customer didn't mention an accident, a branded title, or hidden damage, you're not on the hook for the number. Your Terms, your Privacy Policy, and your Offer Disclosure all carry **your** store's name and are written to keep you covered. Everything is also built to meet Connecticut's privacy law and the federal rules that apply to dealers who arrange financing.

---

## What happens to a customer's information over time

You don't have to manage any of this — it happens on its own:

- **Call recordings & transcripts** are automatically erased after about **90 days**.
- **A customer's offer link** expires after about **90 days**.
- **AI notes** about a customer are cleared after about **180 days**.
- **The lead record itself** is kept for up to about **3 years** for your business and tax records, then handled per policy.
- **Any time a customer asks to be deleted,** everything identifying them is permanently wiped and the files removed.

So a customer's information is protected while it's useful to you, quietly minimized as it ages, deletable on request, and gone when your relationship with us ends.

You can say that to any customer and mean every word of it. And when your IT person or attorney wants the technical details, we have a full companion brief ready for them — with the specifics, the compliance mapping, and confirmation that all of this was independently reviewed and verified.
