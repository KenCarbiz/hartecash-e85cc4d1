The Three Promises

What this brief actually commits to.

Read these three sentences. If HarteCash doesn't deliver every one of them, the platform fails its own contract.

01

The dealership owns the customer.

Every lead, every phone number, every VIN, every offer accepted or rejected belongs to the rooftop. HarteCash is the data processor. Harte INFINITI is the data controller. Full stop.

02

Full data export to your CRM on demand, on schedule, or on termination. No hostage data, no API ransom, no "we'll get back to you in 90 days." That's written into the DPA.

Lifecycle & Archival

What happens to a customer record after the deal ends.

Every record has a defined exit. Nothing sits indefinitely in a black box. The recommended flow keeps the record inside HarteCash during the active acquisition window, then hands it back to the dealership CRM with full notes attached.

Outcome 1

Deal Transacted

Vehicle acquired. Full deal jacket — including all communications, offer history, and inspection — exports to the CRM and the record is archived in HarteCash.

→ Archived to dealership CRM
Outcome 2

Customer Aged Out

If a lead has no engagement for the configured aging window (default 90 days after last touch), the record is automatically archived to the CRM with status "aged out" and removed from active pipeline.

→ Archived to dealership CRM
Outcome 3

Customer Opted Out

Seller tells us they're no longer selling, or hits STOP on SMS. Record is flagged "do not contact," archived to the CRM with disposition notes, and removed from all outbound touches immediately.

→ Archived to dealership CRM
⚠ Recommendation

Do not export to the CRM on day one. Let HarteCash work the 23-day window. The system is purpose-built for private-party seller nurture; the CRM is not. Premature export buries the lead in the same UCT/internet lead pool where most acquisition leads go to die.

How the Data is Protected

Plain English version: bank-grade, in transit and at rest.

If you've ever used online banking, your money sits behind the same class of infrastructure your customers' data sits behind here. Below is the dealer-readable version. The IT-readable version is one section down.

⚙ For IT & Compliance

Technical appendix.

Open this section and hand it to whoever runs the Harte network, the DMS integration, or compliance review. Expand below.

Hosting & runtime

Application is served from Vercel (US edge network, SOC 2 Type II). Long-running services run on Render in the US-East region. Database is hosted on Supabase (managed Postgres 15, AWS US-East-1, SOC 2 Type II).

frontendVercel · Next.js workersRender · Node dbSupabase · Postgres 15 regionAWS US-East-1

Tenant isolation

Multi-tenant architecture with per-tenant tenant_id on every row of every customer-bearing table. Isolation is enforced via Postgres Row Level Security (RLS) policies, not by application code.

This means: even if an application bug attempted to query another tenant's data, Postgres itself would refuse the query at the database layer. RLS policies are version-controlled and reviewable.

  • RLS enabled on all customers, vehicles, offers, messages, deals tables
  • JWT-scoped claims include tenant_id; policies match auth.jwt() -> tenant_id = row.tenant_id
  • Service-role keys (which bypass RLS) are never exposed to client; server-only
  • Each Harte rooftop = one tenant; cross-rooftop joins require explicit group-admin permission

Encryption

In transit: TLS 1.2+ enforced on all endpoints. HTTPS-only cookies, HSTS headers set. No mixed content.

At rest: AES-256 disk encryption via Supabase/AWS RDS managed storage. Database backups encrypted with separate KMS keys.

Secrets: Stored in Vercel environment variables and Render env vaults. No secrets committed to source control (verified via secret scanning + .gitignore).

Authentication & access

Authentication via Supabase Auth with email + password and optional magic-link. bcrypt hashed credentials, never stored plain. Session tokens are short-lived JWTs (1 hour) with refresh-token rotation.

  • Role-based access control (RBAC): owner, manager, desk, buyer, viewer
  • 2FA available; MFA enforcement configurable per tenant
  • Platform-admin access to a tenant requires explicit grant from a tenant owner
  • All admin actions logged to immutable audit_log table

Third-party integrations

Each integration is scoped to least-privilege credentials and called server-side only.

No customer PII is sent to AI providers in identifiable form. VIN-only and condition-only payloads to valuation APIs. SMS payloads go through Twilio under registered campaign Harte INFINITI (CT 860-936-9221).

Data subject rights & retention

Dealership is the data controller; the platform is the processor. The DPA defines the following:

  • Active records retained in HarteCash during the 23-day acquisition window plus a configurable aging buffer (default 90 days)
  • On transaction, opt-out, or aging: record archived to dealership CRM via API or scheduled CSV export
  • On dealership request: full export of all tenant data within 7 business days (CSV + JSON)
  • On contract termination: 30-day grace period for data export, then deletion within 60 days with deletion certificate
  • Customer opt-out (STOP on SMS, email unsubscribe, written request) honored within 24 hours system-wide

Logging, audit, incident response

  • Application logs: 30-day retention (configurable up to 1 year on request)
  • Database audit log: row-level reads and writes on PII-bearing tables, 1-year retention
  • Authentication events: 1-year retention
  • Incident response: any confirmed breach of dealership data disclosed within 72 hours per DPA
  • Penetration testing: annual third-party engagement (report available under NDA)

Compliance posture

  • GLBA Safeguards Rule: HarteCash processes nonpublic personal information under dealership-controlled flows; safeguards documented and reviewable
  • TCPA / A2P 10DLC: Carrier-registered SMS campaigns with documented consent capture and STOP/HELP handling
  • FTC Used Car Rule: HarteCash does not generate vehicle disclosures; that responsibility remains with the dealership
  • State privacy laws: Honors deletion and access requests under CT, NY, NJ, MA, CA frameworks as applicable

Verification & transparency

The Harte IT team is welcome to:

  • Review the live RLS policies via a read-only Supabase dashboard invite
  • Request a sample of the audit log for any 24-hour window
  • Run a penetration test against the tenant (with written notice)
  • Receive SOC 2 reports of all upstream providers under NDA
  • Read the executed DPA in full before any data flows

How Your Offer is Calculated

What you provide

  • VIN, mileage, and your honest assessment of current condition
  • Photos of the vehicle — exterior, interior, dashboard, odometer

What our system does

  • Looks up your vehicle's Black Book wholesale value
  • Pulls recent comparable sales in our market
  • Applies condition adjustments based on your photos and report
  • Calculates what the dealership can pay and still profit on retail

What you get back

  • A firm offer good for 7 days
  • Subject to physical inspection at the dealership
  • Subject to clean title (or arranged payoff if there's an existing loan)

What could change the offer

  • Inspection reveals different condition than reported
  • Mechanical or accident issues not visible in photos
  • Title issues (liens, branded titles, etc.)

The bottom line

The offer is calculated from real market data, not a lowball lead-in number designed to get you in the door. If the inspection matches the report, the offer holds.

Data Processing — How It Works

Who's responsible for what

  • Harte INFINITI — the data controller. Decides what to do with customer data.
  • HarteCash — the data processor. Handles it strictly according to dealership instructions.

What HarteCash will never do

  • Sell, license, or share customer data with any third party
  • Use customer data to market products outside of the acquisition process
  • Allow other dealerships to see Harte's data

Subprocessors (who else touches the data)

  • Supabase — database hosting (SOC 2 Type II)
  • Vercel — web hosting (SOC 2 Type II)
  • Twilio — SMS delivery (SOC 2 Type II)
  • Black Book — vehicle valuation, no customer PII shared
  • fal.ai — photo processing, vehicle photos only
  • Anthropic — descriptive text generation, no customer PII

If something goes wrong

  • Confirmed breach disclosed to the dealership within 72 hours
  • Affected customers notified per state law

When it ends

  • Dealership can request a full data export at any time
  • 30-day grace period for export on termination
  • Full deletion within 60 days; deletion certificate provided
